## v0.6.1 - 2026-11-20

v0.6.1 changes only go-socket's own tests and tiers. No package's code, call or behaviour changes from v0.6.0, so a program that uses go-socket has nothing to read here.

### Changed
* The pre-push tier is back under its 10 s budget. The root package's tests build go-task once for the package instead of once per test, and the race binaries its hatch rows run exit without the race detector's 1 s pause. The tier's own race run keeps that pause, and no test, row or refusal is skipped, moved or loosened. A warm push's floor on the darwin-arm64 host is now 5.64 s, down from 6.75 s.

Nothing is added or removed: `API.txt` is the same as v0.6.0's.

Non-test Go lines outside internal/: 3,627, as at v0.6.0, at the ceiling of 3,627.

go-socket v0.6.1 requires what v0.6.0 does, and none of it moved: github.com/Microsoft/go-winio v0.6.4, github.com/bored-engineer/go-launchd v0.0.0-20241220214420-b514778f4f80, github.com/coreos/go-systemd/v22 v22.7.0, github.com/docker/go-connections v0.8.2, github.com/gofrs/flock v0.13.2 and golang.org/x/sys v0.50.0. Its youngest requirement, github.com/gofrs/flock v0.13.2, is 12 days old on 20 November 2026.
